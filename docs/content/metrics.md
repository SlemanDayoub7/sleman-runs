## Weekly review

1. Which post got the most profile views? Post that *kind* again next week.
2. Where did the funnel leak — impressions → views, or views → DMs?
3. Did anything ship that isn't visible in the demo? Make it visible.
4. One thing to cut next week. Always cut something.

## What good looks like by week 8

These are sanity checks, not targets to game.

- 60+ PRs merged, `main` never red
- 150+ tests, domain coverage above 90%
- 1 000+ LinkedIn impressions per engineering post
- 20+ profile views per week
- 5+ real conversations with hiring managers or founders
- 2+ interview processes started
